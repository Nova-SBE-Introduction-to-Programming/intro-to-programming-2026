One complete first release.

Build an experience that works from beginning to end, with sample content, clear rules, and progress that survives an app restart. Every brief includes concrete acceptance scenarios so you can check the result.

You choose the design.

Make the name, setting, content, and interface your own. Meet the required behaviour before adding extensions. You may use AI to build; you must be able to explain and verify what it produces.

Keep the scope manageable.

Support one user at a time. Accounts, payments, multiplayer, external APIs, and LLM features are not required. Build individually in W5–W7 and keep your work on GitHub, ready for W8.

The solo project is examined rather than submitted for a separate grade. Acceptance scenarios are product checks, not the exam question template.
